Likely research papers that could be used for our report:

Paper and explained what to use it for
1. Rafailov et al., “Direct Preference Optimization: Your Language Model is Secretly a Reward Model” (arXiv 2305.18290, 2023)
Your core learning method. I’m fairly sure it was published at NeurIPS 2023, so check.

2. Gao, Yen, Yu, Chen, “Enabling Large Language Models to Generate Text with Citations” (EMNLP 2023, doi 10.18653/v1/2023.emnlp-main.398)	
Citation correctness metrics; closest to your “cite your sources” goal.

3. Lewis et al., “Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks” (NeurIPS 2020)	
The retrieve-then-generate design.

4. Ouyang et al., “Training language models to follow instructions with human feedback” (2022)	
RLHF, the method DPO simplifies.

5. Ji et al., “Survey of Hallucination in Natural Language Generation” (ACM Computing Surveys, 2023)	
Why you abstain and verify claims.

6. Maas et al., “Learning Word Vectors for Sentiment Analysis” (ACL 2011)	
The classic IMDb review dataset, so you can compare it to your data.

7. Tunstall et al., “Zephyr: Direct Distillation of LM Alignment” (arXiv 2023)	
DPO applied to a small open model, like your plan.

8. Nakano et al., “WebGPT: Browser-assisted question-answering with human feedback” (2021)	
Web search plus citations plus human feedback.
